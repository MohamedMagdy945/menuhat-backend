# Menuhat

> Status: Draft
> Version: 0.1

## Product Vision

Menouhat is a food platform that connects customers with restaurants.

Restaurants can register on the platform and manage their menus,
while customers can discover restaurants, browse menus, place orders,
and review restaurants.

## Main Users

- Customer
- Restaurant Owner
- Admin

## Core Capabilities

### Restaurants

- Restaurant registration
- Restaurant management
- Restaurant approval

### Menus

- Create menu items
- Update menu items
- Manage item availability

### Orders

- Customers can place orders
- Restaurants can manage orders
- Customers can track the status of their orders

### Reviews

- Customers can review restaurants
